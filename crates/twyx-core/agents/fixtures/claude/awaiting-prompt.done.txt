
  I type man and the pages open wide,
  Dense columns of the flags that I forgot.
  The knowledge of the elders lives inside,
  In terse plain text that tells me what is what.

  When ssh connects me far away,
  A server in a cold room hums for me.
  The same small prompt is there, the same display,
  And distance falls away to what I see.

  At night the tmux panes stay split and lit,
  A log that tails, a process that still runs.
  I close the lid, but they do not quit;
  They wait for me like patient, quiet ones.

  So let the windows bloom with all their chrome;
  I keep this narrow place where words have weight.
  The terminal, the cursor, and the home:
  A tilde marks the place where I return and wait.
